# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Tan Tan Teatro uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Cookies are small text files stored on the user’s device while browsing and are used to improve the browsing experience.

## Types of cookies used

### Technical cookies

The website uses technical cookies necessary for the correct functioning of pages and services.

### Analytics cookies

Anonymous analytics tools may be used to collect aggregated information about website usage.

### Third-party cookies

The website may include content from external services (such as YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps, or social networks) that may install profiling cookies.

## Consent management

When accessing the website for the first time, users can accept or reject non-essential cookies through the consent banner.

Users may also modify cookie preferences at any time through their browser settings.

## Additional information

For more information about personal data processing, please refer to the Privacy Policy.
